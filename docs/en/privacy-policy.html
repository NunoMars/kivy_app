🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2025

This privacy policy explains how the "Ma Carte de Tarot" Android application collects, uses and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Automatically Collected Data

The app may automatically collect the following information:

  • Usage data: Number of spreads, app sessions and general feature usage.
  • Device data: Device model, operating system version and app version.
  • Ad data: Google advertising ID (GAID) for delivering personalised ads via AdMob.

1.2 Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, address)
  • Precise location data or GPS coordinates
  • Contacts, photos or any sensitive information stored on your device
  • Your tarot reading history or questions

2. How We Use Information

The collected data is used solely to:

  • Improve the app: Analyse anonymous usage to improve user experience and stability.
  • Display ads: Google AdMob uses the advertising ID to deliver relevant ads.
  • Monitor performance: Understand installs, crashes and feature adoption.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app integrates Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial adverts. AdMob may use cookies or unique identifiers to deliver and measure ads.

3.1 Types of Ads

  • Banners: Displayed on the result screen after each draw.
  • Interstitials: Full-screen ads shown periodically (default every 5 draws).

3.2 Ad Personalisation Choices

You can control personalised ads through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Disable ad personalisation
  • More details: https://support.google.com/ads/answer/2662922

3.3 Google Privacy Policy

Read how Google handles data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. App Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to load AdMob ads.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Checks network connectivity before loading ads.
  • AD_ID: Access to the Google advertising ID for personalised ads.

Important: Tarot draws work offline. An internet connection is only needed to display ads.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. Data is only shared with:

  • Google AdMob: For advertising delivery in accordance with their policy.
  • Google Play Services: To support core Android functionality.

6. Data Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

7. Data Retention

AdMob retains data according to Google's policies. We do not store personal data on our own servers.

8. Your Rights

You can:

  • Disable personalised ads via your device settings.
  • Uninstall the app to stop any data collection.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be published here along with the new effective date.
