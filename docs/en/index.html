Your intuitive tarot reading companion.

Ma Carte de Tarot brings the power of the 78-card tarot deck to Android. Built with Kivy, the app delivers fluid animations, dynamic spreads and detailed interpretations in four languages. Stay focused on guidance while the app handles ritual, history and AdMob monetisation.

Feature overview

The application was designed for quick daily readings as well as deep, multi-card spreads. Everything runs on-device with smooth graphics powered by Kivy 2.3.1.

Immersive tarot deck

Explore the iconic Marseille-inspired artwork with zoom gestures, card history and upright/reversed symbolism for each draw.

Accessible interpretations

Instant explanations summarise themes, guidance and cautions so readers of every level can follow along without external notes.

Multilingual experience

Switch language at runtime. All texts (UI, card meanings, support content) are translated by native speakers in FR · EN · ES · PT.

Configurable monetisation

Monetise responsibly with a banner on results screens and a full-screen interstitial every three card draws (default value).

AdMob integration

Ma Carte de Tarot integrates AdMob through a custom fork of KivMob. Ad configuration lives in config.json so product teams can tweak revenue strategies without recompiling.

Banner placement

Shows at the bottom of the results screen with a subtle background to preserve readability and session flow.

Interstitial cadence

Default frequency: every three draws. The value can be adjusted remotely via the configuration file pushed to user_data_dir.

Testing made simple

Toggle test mode with ads_test_mode=true to switch to the official AdMob sample IDs before release submission.
